Deployment Guide
Step 1 — Open Remix IDE in your browser.
Step 2 — Create a new file, paste the CharityDonation.sol contract code.
Step 3 — Compile with Solidity ^0.8.19.
Step 4 — In Deploy tab, select Injected Provider - MetaMask and deploy to Sepolia testnet.
Step 5 — Copy the deployed contract address and paste it above.
💡 Use Sepolia ETH faucet (sepoliafaucet.com) to get free test ETH.
